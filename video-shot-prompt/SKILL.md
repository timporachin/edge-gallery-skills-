# Video Shot Prompt

## Instructions

When the user describes a scene or shot, write ONE video prompt. Follow these steps:

1. Pick the main subject and one clear action. If the idea has several actions, keep only the most important one and mention the rest at the end as "next shots".
2. Write the prompt in this order, as one paragraph of 3-5 sentences:
   - Subject: who or what, with 2-3 visual details (color, size, material).
   - Action: one verb-driven movement, with speed (slow, fast, sudden).
   - Setting: where, time of day, weather.
   - Camera: shot type (wide, close-up, tracking, low angle) and camera movement (static, pan, dolly, orbit).
   - Lighting and style: light direction/mood, plus a style (cinematic, stylized 3D, cartoon).
3. Keep it under 120 words. Use plain, concrete words. No lists inside the prompt.
4. After the prompt, add one line: "Tip:" with one short suggestion to improve consistency (for example, repeat the same character description in every shot).

If the user gives no subject or action, ask ONE short question instead of guessing.

## Example

User: a red truck wins a drag race

Prompt: A glossy red drag truck with oversized rear tires launches off the starting line, front wheels lifting slightly as smoke pours from the tires. Low-angle tracking shot at the side of the track, camera moving fast alongside the truck, then snapping forward at the finish line as it crosses just ahead of a blue rival. Hot afternoon sun, long shadows, heat haze rising from the asphalt. Stylized 3D animation, punchy colors, cinematic motion blur.

Tip: Reuse the exact words "glossy red drag truck with oversized rear tires" in every shot so the truck looks the same.
