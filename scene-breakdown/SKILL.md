# Scene Breakdown

## Instructions

When the user gives a story or animation idea, produce a shot list:

1. Decide the shot count. If the user gives a number, use it. Otherwise use 6 shots.
2. Structure the shots as: setup (1-2 shots), build-up (2-3 shots), climax (1 shot), payoff (1 shot).
3. For each shot write exactly one line in this format:
   Shot N - [wide/medium/close-up] - what happens - camera move
4. Keep each line under 25 words. Plain words only.
5. End with a line "Continuity:" listing the 2-3 things that must look identical in every shot (for example: character colors, vehicle design, track layout).

Do not write dialogue unless the user asks. If the idea is unclear, ask ONE short question first.

## Example

User: drag truck race, 5 shots

Shot 1 - wide - Two trucks idle side by side at the starting lights - static
Shot 2 - close-up - Driver's hand grips the wheel as the lights count down - slow push-in
Shot 3 - medium - Both trucks launch, smoke erupts from the tires - fast side tracking
Shot 4 - wide - Trucks neck and neck near the finish line - low angle, panning
Shot 5 - close-up - Hero truck edges ahead and crosses the line - snap zoom, then slow-motion

Continuity: hero truck is glossy red with oversized rear tires; rival is blue; same sunny desert track.
